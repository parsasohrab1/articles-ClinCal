# Business Requirements Specification – ClinCal

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Quantify how a risk model trained at one hospital is mis-calibrated at other hospitals. |
| BR-2 | Quantify how many labelled local patients are needed for recalibration to help, and when it hurts. |
| BR-3 | Report every result on real patient data, including negative ones. |
| BR-4 | Make no claim of clinical utility; research use only. |

Out of scope: prospective validation, regulatory submission, deployment in a clinical workflow.
